Secondary/primary ON peak ratio: median 0.019 over 41 mapped takes.
Each take averages the first measurement slots. A secondary local peak must lie at least
300 us from the primary with an intervening trough at or below half the secondary height.
Zero denotes no qualifying secondary peak in an active slot. Multiple bursts can share a slot.
The ratio measures waveform shape; optical echo attribution requires independent evidence.

Balance loss is half the sum of absolute channel-share errors (0 best, 1 worst).
Each target share is its Valspar digital R, G or B value divided by their sum.
Signed share bias is prediction minus reference in percentage points. Positive means overrepresented.
RGB MAE is the mean absolute error of three 0–255 channels. A/B/C use Yan's display mapping.
D/E use fitted digital RGB. Calibration holds out the entire swatch and its recordings.
Means weight swatches equally, then recordings equally within swatch. Rejections count toward coverage.
The colour targets approximate paint appearance. The timing proxy uses global ON peaks in firmware
slots (100 us bins). B uses G/B peaks to align. Red peaks are withheld from the phase estimate.
The dark-interval ON fraction in recordings.csv includes delayed responses and background activity.
Independent optical timing and physical paint colour were unavailable.
